## smail.pw vs smailpro (Brand Clarification)

People sometimes search for smail.pw and land on a similarly named service, or the other way round. This page states plainly what smail.pw is, so you can tell whether you are in the right place.

### The short version

- smail.pw is an **independent temporary email service**.
- It is not the same product as smail pro or smailpro, and it is not affiliated with them or with any other similarly named site.
- Its features, policies, and infrastructure are its own. Nothing you do on smail.pw carries over to those services, and nothing from them carries over here.

### How to tell you are on smail.pw

- The address bar shows smail.pw.
- Every address the site generates ends in @smail.pw. There is no other domain to choose from.
- There is no sign-up, no login, and no paid plan. If a page asks you to create an account or to pay, it is not smail.pw.
- The site carries no ads.

### What smail.pw does

- Generates a temporary address in one click, with no registration
- Receives email and shows it on the page as it arrives
- Keeps the same address in your browser until you replace or delete it

It does not send email, and it does not show attachments.

### If you were looking for another service

We cannot help with accounts, addresses, or emails on other services: we have no access to them and no relationship with their operators. For those, use the support channel on the site where you created the address.
