## Temporary Email for Registration

A **temporary email for registration** lets you create an account on a website without giving it your long-term address. The sign-up email, the welcome series, and the marketing that follows all land in the temporary inbox instead of yours.

### Accounts it suits

- Free trials and product demos
- Forums, comment sections, and communities you read occasionally
- Newsletters you want to sample before subscribing for real
- Events and webinars that ask for an email to let you in
- Giveaways and coupon sign-ups that are followed by promotional mail
- Test accounts for developers and QA

### Accounts it does not suit

- Anything with money in it: banks, payment apps, shops where you store a card
- Work, school, and government accounts
- Accounts holding things you paid for, such as games, software licences, or subscriptions
- Your main social and messaging accounts

The test is simple: if losing access to the account would cost you something, register with a permanent mailbox.

### Registering, step by step

1. Generate an address on smail.pw and copy it.
2. Paste it into the sign-up form and submit.
3. Open the confirmation email when it appears in the inbox, then click the link or copy the code.
4. Finish the sign-up. The address stays the same, so later emails from that site keep arriving in the same inbox.

### What happens after you register

On smail.pw the address does not run out on a timer. As long as you use the same browser and do not replace or delete the address, a password-reset or notification email sent next week arrives in the same inbox.

That is useful, but it is not a promise:

- The address lives in this browser's cookies. Another browser or device will not have it, and if the cookies are cleared it cannot be recovered.
- The cookie is renewed each time you open smail.pw. A browser that has not opened the site for more than about a year may drop it, and the address with it.
- Addresses and emails are kept long-term by default, with no guarantee. They may be removed during periodic cleanup, maintenance, or after a system fault.

### If the account turns out to matter

If a trial becomes something you rely on, change the account's email to a permanent address in its settings while you can still receive the confirmation email at the temporary one. Do this before you replace or delete the smail.pw address.

### If the site rejects the address

Some sites refuse temporary email domains at sign-up. Generating another address will not get around it, because every smail.pw address uses the same domain. Use a permanent mailbox or an email alias there.
